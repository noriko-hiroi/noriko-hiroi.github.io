Advanced AI applications

Recent proceedings of the technoloies relating generative AI is incredively fast, and spreading everywhere day by day. We use these technologies with packaged library or from the scrach for providing new medical services and solving uncovered social problems.

Our new challenge has been recently expanded into building an deep learning machine with a series of analog calculators, named PDL(Physical Driven Learning machine) project. This project had been achieved a publication (Chiba T et al., March 2026), and get started the next step.

What's New 1 (20260430)

The newly launched our project is "Self-Evolving Circuits: Developing Autonomous Growth AI Chip Technology" - Experience the future of AI where hardware is no longer static. We are building a next-gen AI substrate that adapts its physical configuration in real-time using the "Free Energy Principle." This growing hardware prototype (FPGA+alpha) optimises itself like a biological brain to minimize redundant computation. Be part of the journeyy to create the first self-maturing hardware architecture. The "Free Energy Principle (FEP) driven Partial Reconfiguration" project initiated in April 2026.

What's New 2 (20260423)
Systems Energetics Project has launched!

We will develop an AI agent which deals a "smart grid" network. We recondtruct the digital twin of an example CEMS, and test our agent.
This project are proceeding by Takenobu Ike (B4) and 7 freshers of Electrical Electronic Informatics Engineering.

Applications of generative AI are supported by the Advanced AI Research Institute of Kanagawa Institute of Technology.

Building analog deep learning machine has gotten a technical support by Microchip Design Lab Ltd.
