Symbiotic Engineering:

an engineering which stands for the solution of the problems of not only humans but also other living creatures with various problems for their survival, and to enable them to live together more successfully than they do now, instead of exploiting other living organisms in pursuit of human desires. For this purpose, we want to reveal unexplored knowledge of life to apply it for the above use but with a different way from taking life apart and examine it for curiosity. We actually start to get the tools for our purpose day by day.
